SOCCERMASTER – GUIDE FOR NEW PLAYERS
=====================================

Welcome to Soccermaster – a retro football manager in C64 style!
You take charge of a Bundesliga club in the 1983/84 season and lead it
through a full season of 34 matchdays. Up to 4 players can compete
simultaneously in the same league.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. GETTING STARTED: LOGIN AND PROFILE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
On your first visit, sign in with your Google account.
Then choose a nickname once – it will be shown to other players
in the lobby. You can change it at any time in the profile menu.

FAVOURITE CLUB:
In your profile you can also set a favourite club.
It will be automatically pre-selected when you join a new lobby –
you just need to confirm it. If two players share the same favourite
club, the result is decided randomly.

PROFILE PICTURE:
After login, your Google profile picture is automatically saved and
displayed in C64 style (16 colours, pixelated retro look).
You can replace it at any time with your own image:
Click the [ AVATAR ] button in the profile area and select a file.
  Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, WebP, BMP
  High-resolution, high-contrast images produce the best results.
The image is converted to C64 style on the server.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2. LOBBY: CREATE OR JOIN A GAME
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CREATE A NEW LOBBY:
Click "Create Lobby". You receive a 6-digit code
that you share with fellow players (e.g. via chat or verbally).

JOIN AN EXISTING LOBBY:
Enter a fellow player's code and click "Join".
Up to 4 players can play in one lobby.

LOAD YOUR OWN SAVES (Solo):
Ongoing solo saves appear automatically under "Ongoing Games".
Click on a game to continue playing solo.

LOAD MULTIPLAYER SAVES:
Ongoing multiplayer games are saved automatically.
The lobby creator can see their saved MP games and load them.
After loading, everyone waits for the registered co-players – anyone
who doesn't show up is replaced by the AI. The creator can also start
without all players. Important: joining an MP save requires a Google
login (guest players are not accepted).

Once all players are ready, team selection begins.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
3. TEAM SELECTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Each player picks a club from the pool of historic
Bundesliga teams (including some East German clubs).

- Each club can only be chosen once
- Unchosen BL1 slots are filled with AI teams
- All teams start with the same fixed capital


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
4. DRAFT: YOUR STARTING SQUAD
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
After team selection, all teams automatically receive 15 players
through a balanced draft. You start with a balanced mix of
goalkeepers, defenders, midfielders and forwards.

The market values of these players are deducted directly from your
starting capital. What remains is your budget for transfers.

Player strengths are NOT shown as numbers – you only see
the category:
  World class → Very strong → Strong → Average → Weak → Very weak

Note: Each player can only be fielded in their natural position.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
5. MANAGEMENT PHASE: YOUR WEEK
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Before each matchday you have a management phase. Here you can:

SQUAD SELECTION:
- Choose your 11 starting players from the squad
- Formation is determined automatically by the positions fielded
- You cannot play players out of their natural position
- Injured and suspended players are greyed out and unavailable

DOMESTIC TRANSFER MARKET:
- Several offers per visit (domestic players)
- Some offers may be shared with other managers →
  first to buy gets the player
- Purchases only possible as long as your balance isn't too far negative

INTERNATIONAL TRANSFER MARKET:
- Exclusive offers with stronger players (internationals possible)
- Only accessible if: balance positive + free slots for internationals
- Occasionally an offer may be shared with another manager

SELLING:
- Emergency sale: instant sale with a discount on market value
- List on market: Full market value, player is offered to other
  managers next week on the domestic market

LISTED PLAYERS:
Players you have listed appear in the squad tab in a separate
"LISTED FOR SALE" section below the bench.
They are marked [MARKET] and cannot be added to the starting XI
while listed.

STATUS:
- Shows injured and suspended players, and those on yellow card warnings

TABLE & FINANCES:
- Current league table and your balance viewable at any time

When you're done, click "START MATCHDAY".
The game waits until all managers are ready, then the matchday begins.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
6. MATCHDAY: THE GAME IS ON
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
All league matches run simultaneously (approx. 1 game minute per second).

FOR YOUR OWN MATCH:
- Full live ticker: goals, cards, injuries with exact minute
- Current scores of all other matches are visible

FOR OTHER MANAGERS' MATCHES:
- Short ticker for goals, cards and injuries

FOR AI MATCHES:
- Running score only, no commentary

FAST FORWARD (admins/testers only):
- The "FAST FORWARD" button appears at the top once a matchday starts.
- After clicking, the match runs through without pauses.
- After the final whistle, all events (goals, cards, injuries)
  are shown as a summary in the ticker.

AFTER THE FINAL WHISTLE:
- Summary of your match (goals, cards, injuries)
- Results of all other matches are viewable
- Click "NEXT" when you're ready for the next week
  (a short timer runs – you don't have to wait for everyone else)

REVENUE:
- Home matches bring gate receipts (the better your league position,
  the more spectators)


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
7. TIPS FOR BEGINNERS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
✓ Check your squad first: Before the matchday verify all 11 starters
  are fit and not suspended. Note in multiplayer: if you change your
  lineup after clicking "Ready", you are automatically set back to
  "Not ready" – other players see this immediately.

✓ Visit the domestic market early: Shared offers are first-come-first-served.
  Whoever looks first secures the best players.

✓ Squad balance: Always keep at least 2 goalkeepers – if the only
  goalkeeper is unavailable, the weakest outfield player takes over
  with a significant strength penalty.

✓ Watch your finances: High wages eat through your budget quickly.
  Sell surplus players on the market early (better price than emergency sale).

✓ Monitor yellow cards: Players close to the suspension threshold risk
  a ban with the next card. Always check in the status screen.

✓ Use internationals wisely: Expensive but strong – one good international
  in the starting XI can make the difference.

✓ Tight budget? No problem: Weaker clubs can still be successful –
  good lineup choices and smart transfers matter more than squad cost.

✓ Watch the youth market: At the end of the season new players enter
  the market. Act early to secure talents before others do.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
8. FINANCES AT A GLANCE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Every week player wages are deducted (proportional share of monthly salary).

INCOME:
  + Gate receipts for home matches
  + European cup revenue (if qualified)
  + Transfer proceeds (sales, market listings)

EXPENSES:
  − Player wages (weekly)
  − Transfers (purchases)

WARNING:
- Balance too far negative → no purchases on the domestic market
- Balance negative at season end → insolvency warning
- Repeatedly negative → dismissal (game over)

Tip: Watch your wage bill! An expensive squad drains your budget fast.
Sometimes a cheaper, solid player is better than an expensive star.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
9. CARDS AND SUSPENSIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
YELLOW CARDS:
- Players accumulate yellow cards throughout the season
- After a certain number in one cycle: automatic one-week ban
- A new counting cycle then begins
- Yellow-red card (2× yellow in one match): immediate dismissal,
  multi-week ban

The squad screen shows e.g.:
  "Hans Mueller  Strong  –1–  3(1)"
  → 3 yellow cards total, 1 in current cycle until next ban

Cup matches do NOT count towards the suspension cycle (league only).


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
10. INJURIES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- Players can get injured during a match
- Absence: a few weeks to several months (depending on diagnosis)
- Injured players appear in the status screen and cannot be fielded
- Injury weeks decrease automatically by 1 each game week

Example diagnoses (mild to severe):
  Mild strain → Bone bruise → Torn ligament


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
11. FOREIGNERS RULE (1980s STYLE)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Historically accurate: maximum 3 international players allowed.

- Maximum 3 internationals in the squad and starting XI
- International players are identified by the [I] badge

Internationals are often stronger than domestic players (domestic
players are capped at the top tier), but market prices are
correspondingly higher.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
12. EUROPEAN CUPS & DFB-POKAL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
EUROPEAN CUPS:
If your team qualifies, you take part in one of three
European competitions:

  ECL (Champions Cup):  For the Bundesliga champion
  Cup Winners' Cup:     For the DFB-Pokal winner
  UEFA Cup:             For the top league positions

European matches take place after the respective league matchday.
You receive a notification when your cup match is due and must
click "Next" afterwards as well.
Home and away legs, extra time and penalty shootouts are possible.

DFB-POKAL:
The DFB-Pokal runs alongside the league. All BL1 teams participate,
including AI teams and opponents from Bundesliga 2 and the Oberliga.
Cup matches do not count towards the yellow card cycle of the league.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
13. SEASON WRAP-UP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
At the end of each season several things happen automatically:

PLAYER DEVELOPMENT:
All players develop – positively or negatively. No player stays the same.
Development is random and not predictable for you.

YOUTH INTAKE:
A group of new youth players enters the transfer market.
These include domestic talents and some internationals. They are
mostly in the average-to-good range and refresh the market.

FINANCES:
- League position influences gate receipts for the coming season
- Anyone who finishes the season with a negative balance receives a warning


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
14. INTERFACE, CHAT & EMOJIS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
NAVIGATION:
All actions via mouse click. Top navigation bar:
  SQUAD | DOMESTIC | INTL. | STATUS | TABLE | FIXTURES | FINANCES | MATCHDAY

CHAT:
There are two chat areas:
  Global Chat:  Visible to all logged-in players (lobby area)
  Session Chat: Only visible to players in the same lobby (in-game)

You can switch between areas using the tabs in the chat window.

@MENTIONS:
Type @ in chat to mention other players. The name auto-completes.
A mentioned player receives a notification even if they have
another tab open. Clicking a mention opens that player's profile.

LOBBY CODES IN CHAT:
When someone posts a lobby code, it appears as a clickable link.
Once the lobby no longer exists, the link automatically reverts
to plain text – the code is no longer usable.

MULTIPLAYER: LOBBY HOST LEAVES:
When the creator of an MP lobby leaves the session, the game is
automatically saved and all other players are returned to the main
menu. The save is kept and can be reloaded later.

EMOJIS:
Type : (colon) in chat – a selection of all available emojis appears.
Continue typing to filter. Select by clicking, arrow keys or Tab/Enter.
Classic smileys like :) :-D ;) :( :o :P are automatically converted on send.
Hover over an emoji in chat to see its code as a tooltip.
